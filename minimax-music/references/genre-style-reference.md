# Genre & Style Reference Encyclopedia

> Comprehensive genre guide with typical BPM ranges, instrumentation, key characteristics, and recommended prompt descriptors.

---

## Pop Family

### Pop
- **BPM:** 100–130
- **Key:** Major keys dominant
- **Instruments:** Synths, drum machines, bass, guitars, layered vocals
- **Production:** Polished, compressed, radio-ready
- **Prompt:** `Pop, catchy, upbeat, polished production, layered vocals, danceable, radio-friendly`

### Synthpop
- **BPM:** 110–130
- **Key:** Both major and minor
- **Instruments:** Analog synthesizers, drum machines (LinnDrum, 808), arpeggiators, vocoder
- **Production:** 80s-inspired, lush reverb, gated drums
- **Prompt:** `Synthpop, retro, analog synthesizers, lush pads, gated drums, 80s production, nostalgic`

### Dream Pop
- **BPM:** 80–110
- **Key:** Major/minor, ambiguous
- **Instruments:** Reverb-soaked guitars, ethereal synths, breathy vocals, minimal drums
- **Production:** Washed out, spacious, shoegaze-influenced
- **Prompt:** `Dream pop, ethereal, floating, reverb-washed guitars, breathy vocals, atmospheric, hazy`

### Hyperpop
- **BPM:** 140–180
- **Key:** Any, often distorted
- **Instruments:** Pitched vocals, distorted 808s, glitchy synths, maximalist production
- **Production:** Over-compressed, abrasive, digital, chaotic
- **Prompt:** `Hyperpop, maximalist, glitchy, pitched vocals, distorted bass, chaotic energy, digital`

### K-Pop
- **BPM:** 100–140
- **Key:** Major, frequent modulations
- **Instruments:** Synths, trap beats, orchestral stabs, layered group vocals
- **Production:** Maximalist, genre-blending, dynamic
- **Prompt:** `K-pop, energetic, polished, genre-blending, dynamic shifts, layered vocals, catchy hook`

## Rock Family

### Indie Rock
- **BPM:** 110–140
- **Key:** Major and minor
- **Instruments:** Guitars (clean/overdriven), bass, live drums, vocals
- **Production:** Raw to mid-fi, organic
- **Prompt:** `Indie rock, guitar-driven, raw energy, live drums, authentic, jangly guitars`

### Post-Punk
- **BPM:** 120–150
- **Key:** Minor, dissonant
- **Instruments:** Angular guitars, prominent bass, mechanical drums, spoken/shouted vocals
- **Production:** Dry, stark, tense
- **Prompt:** `Post-punk, angular guitars, driving bass, stark production, tense atmosphere, mechanical drums`

### Shoegaze
- **BPM:** 80–120
- **Key:** Major/minor, blurred
- **Instruments:** Layers of distorted guitar, bass, buried vocals, heavy reverb
- **Production:** Wall of sound, washed out, loud
- **Prompt:** `Shoegaze, wall of sound, distorted guitars, buried vocals, massive reverb, noisy, beautiful`

### Post-Rock
- **BPM:** 60–140 (varies dramatically)
- **Key:** Minor, modal
- **Instruments:** Guitars, bass, drums, strings, piano — builds from quiet to loud
- **Production:** Dynamic, cinematic, spacious
- **Prompt:** `Post-rock, cinematic build, quiet to loud dynamics, tremolo guitar, strings, emotional crescendo`

### Psychedelic Rock
- **BPM:** 80–120
- **Key:** Modal, raga-influenced
- **Instruments:** Fuzz guitars, organ, sitar, tape echo, phased drums
- **Production:** Trippy, effects-heavy, analog warmth
- **Prompt:** `Psychedelic rock, fuzz guitars, tape echo, swirling organ, trippy atmosphere, 60s production`

## Electronic Family

### Ambient
- **BPM:** N/A (often beatless)
- **Key:** Any, often modal/pad-based
- **Instruments:** Synthesizer pads, field recordings, processed acoustic sounds, no drums
- **Production:** Spacious, evolving, minimal
- **Prompt:** `Ambient, beatless, evolving pads, spacious, meditative, textural, warm drones`

### Lo-fi Hip-Hop
- **BPM:** 70–90
- **Key:** Minor, jazzy extensions
- **Instruments:** Sampled piano/guitar, vinyl crackle, boom-bap drums, simple bass
- **Production:** Warm, dusty, compressed
- **Prompt:** `Lo-fi hip-hop, chill, warm vinyl crackle, jazzy samples, relaxed drums, studying music`

### Techno
- **BPM:** 125–140
- **Key:** Minor, atonal
- **Instruments:** Roland TR-909, synthesizers, samplers, minimal vocals
- **Production:** Repetitive, hypnotic, warehouse sound
- **Prompt:** `Techno, driving, hypnotic, 909 drums, dark warehouse, repetitive, industrial textures`

### House
- **BPM:** 118–130
- **Key:** Major and minor
- **Instruments:** Roland TR-909/808, piano stabs, bassline, vocal samples
- **Production:** Four-on-the-floor, soulful, club-ready
- **Prompt:** `House, four-on-the-floor, soulful, piano stabs, grooving bassline, club energy`

### Drum and Bass
- **BPM:** 160–180
- **Key:** Minor
- **Instruments:** Breakbeats (Amen break), sub-bass, synthesizers, samples
- **Production:** Fast, aggressive/atmospheric, heavy bass
- **Prompt:** `Drum and bass, fast breakbeats, deep sub-bass, atmospheric pads, high energy, 170 BPM`

### Synthwave
- **BPM:** 80–110
- **Key:** Minor
- **Instruments:** Analog synths, gated reverb drums, arpeggiated bass, saxophone
- **Production:** 80s retro, neon, cinematic
- **Prompt:** `Synthwave, retro, neon atmosphere, pulsing bass, gated drums, 80s nostalgia, cinematic`

### IDM (Intelligent Dance Music)
- **BPM:** Variable
- **Key:** Complex harmonies
- **Instruments:** Glitchy drums, complex synthesis, processed samples
- **Production:** Experimental, detailed, cerebral
- **Prompt:** `IDM, experimental, glitchy drums, complex rhythms, cerebral, detailed production, abstract`

## Hip-Hop & R&B Family

### Boom Bap
- **BPM:** 85–100
- **Key:** Minor, sampled
- **Instruments:** Sampled breaks, vinyl chops, upright bass, piano loops
- **Production:** Dusty, raw, 90s NYC
- **Prompt:** `Boom bap, dusty drums, vinyl samples, 90s hip-hop, raw, jazzy loops`

### Trap
- **BPM:** 130–160 (half-time feel: 65–80)
- **Key:** Minor
- **Instruments:** 808 bass, fast hi-hats, dark synths, ad-libs
- **Production:** Heavy bass, crisp, modern
- **Prompt:** `Trap, heavy 808s, fast hi-hats, dark atmosphere, triplet flow, modern production`

### Neo-Soul
- **BPM:** 70–95
- **Key:** Jazzy, extended chords
- **Instruments:** Rhodes piano, bass guitar, live drums, smooth vocals, horns
- **Production:** Warm, organic, sophisticated
- **Prompt:** `Neo-soul, warm, jazzy chords, Rhodes piano, smooth vocals, organic drums, sophisticated`

### Alternative R&B
- **BPM:** 60–90
- **Key:** Minor, atmospheric
- **Instruments:** Minimal beats, atmospheric synths, processed vocals, sparse bass
- **Production:** Spacious, moody, experimental
- **Prompt:** `Alternative R&B, atmospheric, sparse, moody, processed vocals, minimal beats, late night`

## Folk & Country Family

### Indie Folk
- **BPM:** 70–110
- **Key:** Major and minor
- **Instruments:** Acoustic guitar, banjo, mandolin, violin, harmonized vocals
- **Production:** Organic, warm, intimate
- **Prompt:** `Indie folk, acoustic, warm harmonies, fingerpicked guitar, intimate, storytelling, organic`

### Americana
- **BPM:** 70–120
- **Key:** Major and minor
- **Instruments:** Acoustic/electric guitars, pedal steel, harmonica, piano, bass
- **Production:** Roots, earthy, live feel
- **Prompt:** `Americana, rootsy, pedal steel, harmonica, live band feel, storytelling, heartland`

### Bluegrass
- **BPM:** 110–160
- **Key:** Major
- **Instruments:** Banjo, fiddle, mandolin, acoustic guitar, upright bass, high harmonies
- **Production:** Live, fast, virtuosic
- **Prompt:** `Bluegrass, fast picking, banjo, fiddle, high harmonies, virtuosic, driving rhythm`

### Singer-Songwriter
- **BPM:** 60–100
- **Key:** Both
- **Instruments:** Acoustic guitar or piano, minimal accompaniment, focus on voice
- **Production:** Minimal, intimate, warm
- **Prompt:** `Singer-songwriter, intimate, acoustic, vulnerable vocals, minimal production, personal`

## Jazz Family

### Cool Jazz
- **BPM:** 60–120
- **Key:** Complex, extended harmonies
- **Instruments:** Trumpet, saxophone, piano, bass, drums — restrained
- **Production:** Clean, spacious, 1950s
- **Prompt:** `Cool jazz, relaxed, understated, brushed drums, walking bass, muted trumpet, sophisticated`

### Jazz Fusion
- **BPM:** 100–150
- **Key:** Complex, modal
- **Instruments:** Electric guitar, synthesizers, saxophone, electric bass, drums
- **Production:** Energetic, technical, 1970s
- **Prompt:** `Jazz fusion, energetic, electric, complex rhythms, synthesizer, saxophone, virtuosic`

### Nu Jazz / Jazz-Hop
- **BPM:** 80–110
- **Key:** Jazzy, sampled
- **Instruments:** Sampled jazz, electronic beats, Rhodes, upright bass, trumpet
- **Production:** Hybrid acoustic/electronic
- **Prompt:** `Nu jazz, jazzy samples, electronic beats, Rhodes piano, laid-back groove, sophisticated`

## Classical & Cinematic Family

### Neoclassical
- **BPM:** Variable
- **Key:** Minor, modal
- **Instruments:** Piano, strings, minimal electronics, sparse
- **Production:** Clean, intimate, concert hall
- **Prompt:** `Neoclassical, piano, strings, minimalist, emotional, sparse, contemporary classical`

### Orchestral / Epic
- **BPM:** 60–140
- **Key:** Minor for drama, major for triumph
- **Instruments:** Full orchestra: strings, brass, woodwinds, percussion, choir
- **Production:** Cinematic, dynamic, huge
- **Prompt:** `Cinematic orchestral, epic, full strings, powerful brass, dramatic percussion, choir, trailer music`

### Minimalism
- **BPM:** Variable
- **Key:** Modal, diatonic
- **Instruments:** Repetitive patterns, gradual transformation, any instrumentation
- **Production:** Clean, precise, hypnotic
- **Prompt:** `Minimalist, repetitive patterns, gradual evolution, hypnotic, precise, Philip Glass-inspired`

## World Music Family

### Afrobeats
- **BPM:** 100–120
- **Key:** Major, pentatonic
- **Instruments:** Percussion, guitars, synthesizers, horns, call-and-response vocals
- **Production:** Rhythmic, danceable, modern
- **Prompt:** `Afrobeats, rhythmic percussion, grooving bass, horns, danceable, energetic, celebratory`

### Bossa Nova
- **BPM:** 100–140
- **Key:** Major, jazz extensions
- **Instruments:** Nylon guitar, piano, light percussion, soft vocals
- **Production:** Warm, intimate, 1960s
- **Prompt:** `Bossa nova, gentle, nylon guitar, soft percussion, warm vocals, Brazilian, relaxed`

### Reggae
- **BPM:** 60–90
- **Key:** Major and minor
- **Instruments:** Guitar skank, bass, drums (one-drop), organ, horns
- **Production:** Laid-back, warm, 1970s
- **Prompt:** `Reggae, laid-back, skanking guitar, deep bass, one-drop drums, warm, island vibe`

## Metal Family

### Heavy Metal
- **BPM:** 100–160
- **Key:** Minor, power chords
- **Instruments:** Distorted guitars, bass, powerful drums, high vocals
- **Production:** Loud, powerful, 80s
- **Prompt:** `Heavy metal, distorted guitars, powerful drums, high vocals, driving riffs, 80s metal`

### Black Metal
- **BPM:** 120–200
- **Key:** Minor, dissonant
- **Instruments:** Tremolo-picked guitars, blast beats, shrieked vocals, atmospheric synths
- **Production:** Raw, lo-fi, cold
- **Prompt:** `Black metal, tremolo guitars, blast beats, shrieked vocals, cold atmosphere, raw production`

### Doom Metal
- **BPM:** 40–80
- **Key:** Minor, sludge
- **Instruments:** Down-tuned guitars, slow drums, bass-heavy, growled/clean vocals
- **Production:** Heavy, crushing, warm
- **Prompt:** `Doom metal, slow, crushing, down-tuned guitars, heavy bass, oppressive atmosphere`

| Mood | BPM Range | Genres |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Relaxed/Calm | 40–80 | Ambient, Doom, Ballads, Lo-fi |
| Grooving/Laid-back | 80–100 | Hip-Hop, R&B, Reggae, Neo-Soul |
| Walking/Mid-tempo | 100–120 | Pop, Rock, House, Afrobeats |
| Energetic/Uplifting | 120–140 | Dance, Synthpop, Indie Rock, Techno |
| Intense/Fast | 140–180 | DnB, Punk, Hyperpop, Black Metal |
| Extreme | 180+ | Speed Metal, Hardcore, Gabber |
